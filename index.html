    Free online radio with no ads

    Free No-Ads Radio is a simple player for live internet radio. Pick any country, search for a station or browse by genre, and press play. It works in any browser on desktop, iPhone and Android, with no app to install, no account and no tracking.

    The directory holds more than 30,000 FM, news, talk and music stations from over 200 countries, from BBC and NPR to local stations in Germany, Turkey, Brazil, India, Iran and Japan. Popular stations are listed first, and your favorites and last station are saved on your device.

    Questions

    Is it really free and ad-free?

    Yes. The player shows no ads and needs no account. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast itself, and no radio app can remove those.

    How do I listen to radio from another country?

    Tap the country button, type the country name, and pick it. The most popular stations in that country appear first.

    Can I use it on iPhone like an app?

    Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen and you can control playback from the lock screen.

    Why is a station missing?

    Only stations with secure (HTTPS) streams are shown, because browsers block insecure audio on secure pages. Stations that are offline are hidden too.
