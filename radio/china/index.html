    Live radio from China

    This page lists 255 radio stations from China that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in China?

    Right now the most listened China stations here are CCTV-13新闻伴音, CNR-1 中国之声, 怀集音乐之声, 新闻联播 and 国际新闻. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to China radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
