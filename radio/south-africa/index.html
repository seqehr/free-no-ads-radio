    Live radio from South Africa

    This page lists 157 radio stations from South Africa that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in South Africa?

    Right now the most listened South Africa stations here are Ukhozi FM S.A, Lesedi, Jacaranda FM, KFM 94.5 and ThobelaFM. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to South Africa radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
