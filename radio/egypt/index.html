    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in Egypt?

    Right now the most listened Egypt stations here are إذاعة محمود خليل الحصري, إذاعة مشاري العفاسي, On sports FM, Nogoum FM and OM Kalthom أم كلثوم. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Egypt radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
