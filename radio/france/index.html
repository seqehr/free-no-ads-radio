    Live radio from France

    This page lists 197 radio stations from France that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Popular formats in France include news, talk, rock, pop, electro and funk. Many stations broadcast from Paris, Vallée Du Rhone, France Continentale, Grand Est and Provence-alpes-côte D'azur, Cannes. Main broadcast languages: French, English and Arabic.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in France?

    Right now the most listened France stations here are RTL, Europe 1, RMC FR, France Inter and Nostalgie HQ. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to France radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
