    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in Portugal?

    Right now the most listened Portugal stations here are RFM, Rádio Comercial, TSF Rádio Notícias, Radio Comercial Portugal and M80 Rádio – 80s. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Portugal radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
