    Live radio from Portugal

    This page lists 211 radio stations from Portugal that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in Portugal?

    Right now the most listened Portugal stations here are RFM, TSF Rádio Notícias, M80 Rádio – 80s, Rádio Comercial and M80 Rádio – Ballads. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Portugal radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
