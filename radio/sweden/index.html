    Live radio from Sweden

    This page lists 168 radio stations from Sweden that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in Sweden?

    Right now the most listened Sweden stations here are Sveriges Radio - P3, Mix Megapol 104,3, Sveriges Radio P1, Sveriges Radio P4 Stockholm and Sveriges Radio P4 Göteborg. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Sweden radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
