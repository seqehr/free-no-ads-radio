    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in Sweden?

    Right now the most listened Sweden stations here are Mix Megapol 104,3, Sveriges Radio - P3, Sveriges Radio - P1, RIX FM 106,7 and Bandit Metal. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Sweden radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
