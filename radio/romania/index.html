    What are the most popular radio stations in Romania?

    Right now the most listened Romania stations here are Magic Fm, Pescobar Radio, Kiss Fm, Atmospheric dnb s0urce and Kiss Fm Ro. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Romania radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
