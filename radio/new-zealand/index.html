    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in New Zealand?

    Right now the most listened New Zealand stations here are 新西兰中文广播电台 FM90.6 Radio Chinese, The Rock - NZ's #1 rock radio station, Newstalk ZB, The Breeze 93.4 and The Sound. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to New Zealand radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
