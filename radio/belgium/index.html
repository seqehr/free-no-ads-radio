    Live radio from Belgium

    This page lists 183 radio stations from Belgium that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in Belgium?

    Right now the most listened Belgium stations here are Qmusic Belgium, JOE, RTBF La Première, Tomorrowland One World Radio and Nostalgie Vlaanderen. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Belgium radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
