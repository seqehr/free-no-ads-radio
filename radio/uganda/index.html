    What are the most popular radio stations in Uganda?

    Right now the most listened Uganda stations here are Galaxy FM 100.2 FM ZZinna!, 98.8 Buddu Fm, 91.2 Crooze FM, NRG Uganda 106.5 and RADIOCITY.UG. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Uganda radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
