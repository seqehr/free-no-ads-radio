    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in the United Kingdom?

    Right now the most listened UK stations here are Heart 80s, CAPITAL - The UK's No.1 Hit Music Station, talkSPORT, Gold and Heart 90s. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to UK radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
