    Live radio from Denmark

    This page lists 114 radio stations from Denmark that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Popular formats in Denmark include pop, regional radio, talk, news, danish and hits. Many stations broadcast from København, Copenhagen, Herning, North Jutland and Fyn. Main broadcast languages: Danish, English and German.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in Denmark?

    Right now the most listened Denmark stations here are Nova FM, DR P3 (AAC), The Voice - Danmarks Hitstation, Classic Rock Danmark and Nova 100% Dansk. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Denmark radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
