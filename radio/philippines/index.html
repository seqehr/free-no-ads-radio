    Live radio from the Philippines

    This page lists 205 radio stations from the Philippines that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in the Philippines?

    Right now the most listened Philippines stations here are Star FM Manila, Christmas Radio Philippines - Powered By www.amfmph.net, 90.7 Love Radio - DZMB, 101.1 Yes FM Manila and Easy Rock Manila. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to Philippines radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
