    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in India?

    Right now the most listened India stations here are Bollywood Gaane Purane, Hindi Gold Radio, Diverse FM, BIG 92.7 FM and Goldy Evergreen. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to India radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
