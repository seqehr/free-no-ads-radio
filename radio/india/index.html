    Live radio from India

    This page lists 317 radio stations from India that stream live over a secure connection, so they play in any modern browser and on iPhone and Android. Stations are ordered by how often listeners play them, and the list is refreshed every week.

    Questions

    What are the most popular radio stations in India?

    Right now the most listened India stations here are KJ Yesudas Malayalam radio, AR RADIO, Radio Malayalam 98.6 FM, Raagam AIR 24*7 and Shahimsha Online radio. The list is ordered by listener clicks and updated every week.

    Can I listen to India radio from abroad?

    Yes. These stations stream over the internet, so you can play them from any country in your browser, with no app and no account.

    Is it free and without ads?

    The player itself is free and shows no ads. Some stations air their own commercials on the broadcast, which no app can remove.
